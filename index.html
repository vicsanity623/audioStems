💡 Prompt Guide & Settings ▾

The reason your previous attempts likely haven't produced good results is that AI models are naturally "stubborn" in img2img. If the model sees a masculine face, it tries to preserve those features, often resulting in a picture of a man awkwardly wearing makeup rather than a true transformation.

To fix this, you need three things: a highly specific positive prompt, a ruthless negative prompt, and the right settings (denoising strength).

1. “Subtle Feminization” Prompt (Gender Swap)

Use when you want a natural, beautiful female version of the subject.

Positive:

A beautiful woman, stunning realistic portrait, long luscious wavy hair, subtle elegant feminine makeup, soft flawless skin, wispy eyelashes, soft pink lipstick, subtle blended eyeshadow. Delicate feminine facial features, soft jawline, gorgeous aesthetic, natural soft lighting, 8k resolution, highly detailed, photorealistic, Vogue editorial photography.

Negative (Critical):

masculine, male, man, stubble, 5 o'clock shadow, facial hair, mustache, beard, Adam's apple, broad jawline, harsh shadows, rough skin, manly, deep wrinkles, ugly, deformed.

2. “RuPaul Drag” Transformation

Use for high-glamor, theatrical, fierce drag queen makeup.

Positive:

A fierce glamorous high-fashion drag queen, RuPaul's Drag Race style, extreme theatrical drag makeup, heavy dramatic eyelashes, sharp cut-crease eyeshadow, heavily contoured cheekbones, bold glossy overlined lips, massive voluminous long luscious wavy hair, dazzling sequin outfit, highly detailed face, professional studio lighting, hyperrealistic, ultra-detailed, drag excellence.

Negative:

masculine, male face, stubble, facial hair, beard, unstyled hair, subtle makeup, bare face, no makeup, natural look, Adam's apple, manly jaw, plain, boring.

💡 The Secret Sauce: Workflow & Settings

A good text prompt is only 30% of the battle.

Denoising Strength (img2img)

  • Too low (0.1–0.4): original guy with smudged colors
  • Too high (0.8–1.0): completely different person
  • Sweet spot: 0.55 – 0.70

CFG Scale

Turn up to 7.5 – 9 so the AI listens strictly to your prompt.

Pro Tips

  • If 5 o'clock shadow persists, blur/smooth the beard area on the original photo before uploading.
  • Generating at higher step counts (20–50) produces more refined detail.
  • Use the Reference Image upload + Remixing Strength slider above to dial in the right blend.
  • ControlNet models must be downloaded separately. Run:
    huggingface-cli download diffusers/controlnet-canny-sdxl-1.0
    huggingface-cli download diffusers/controlnet-depth-sdxl-1.0

The Self-Hosted AI
Media Server Revolution

Four powerful AI models running directly on private hardware — no cloud subscriptions, no data leaving your network. Background removal via InSPyReNet, upscaling through RealESRGAN, audio stem separation with Meta Demucs, and image generation via Stable Diffusion, all orchestrated through a single Tailscale-secured endpoint.

Processing Topology

graph LR Client[Browser / PWA] --> TS{Tailscale Funnel} TS --> iMac[Intel iMac Node] iMac --> BG[Background Removal] iMac --> UP[Upscaler] iMac --> DM[Demucs Audio] iMac --> SD[Stable Diffusion] style TS fill:#00f2ff,color:#000,stroke:none style iMac fill:#111,color:#555,stroke:#333

Each model runs locally on the same Intel iMac with an AMD Radeon Pro 5300 GPU. The server is exposed via Tailscale Funnel (HTTPS), ensuring all processing happens on your own hardware with no third-party inference.

Why Self-Host?

While cloud AI platforms offer convenience, they come at the cost of privacy, recurring subscription fees, and usage limits. This server runs entirely on dedicated hardware — an Intel-based iMac with 40GB of RAM and a dedicated AMD GPU. Background removal, upscaling, Demucs stem separation, and Stable Diffusion inference all execute locally, accessed securely through Tailscale Funnel from any browser worldwide. No data leaves this machine. Check the README.md for steps on how to clone this repo and host it on your own hardware.

The Stack: Python 3.10 + PyTorch 2.2.2 running on macOS. The Gradio-based server orchestrates all four model pipelines behind a unified interface. Tailscale Funnel provides TLS termination and global routing, while Firebase manages authentication and usage statistics.
